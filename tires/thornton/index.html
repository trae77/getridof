How to get rid of tires in Thornton, CO

Checked on Oct 21, 2026 against the official sources linked below.

Short answer: Thornton's city trash programs don't take tires. That includes Click & Haul, Landfill Day and the recycling drop-off sites. Take them to a registered tire recycler instead. Colorado Tire Recyclers in Denver lists passenger tires at $3.25 each ($9.00 on the rim), open for drop-off Monday–Friday, 8am–3:30pm (last drop).

What Thornton city services won't take

  • Click & Haul bulky-item pickup ($72 per collection for 6 cubic yards): "NO tires." City of Thornton
  • Thornton Landfill Day at Republic Services Tower Landfill: tires are not accepted. City of Thornton
  • Recycling drop-off bins (88th & Colorado Blvd, and 12450 Washington St): auto parts and non-listed items aren't accepted. City of Thornton

Other options

  • Use Thornton's Waste Wizard, or call Environmental Services at 720-977-6200 (source).
  • If you're buying new tires, ask the shop whether it takes your old ones. Fees vary, so call to confirm.
  • Have a lot of tires or no way to haul them? Colorado Tire Recyclers also offers pickup. Call to confirm the price.

FAQ

Can I put tires in my Thornton trash can?

The city's Click & Haul and Landfill Day pages both exclude tires. Use the Waste Wizard or call 720-977-6200 for regular cart rules.

How much does it cost to recycle a car tire near Thornton?

Colorado Tire Recyclers lists $3.25 per passenger tire (15–17"), or $9.00 if it's still on the rim (checked Oct 21, 2026).

Do I need an appointment?

Only for more than 50 tires, according to Colorado Tire Recyclers.
