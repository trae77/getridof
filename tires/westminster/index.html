How to get rid of tires in Westminster, CO

Checked on Oct 23, 2026 against the official sources linked below.

Short answer: The City of Westminster doesn't run trash pickup, and the Westminster Sustainability Center (6020 W 91st Ave) lists only bike tires and tubes ($1.50 each), not car tires. For car and truck tires, the closest registered recycler is Colorado Tire Recyclers at 11919 E 37th Ave in northeast Denver, which lists passenger tires at $3.25 each ($9.00 on the rim), open for drop-off Monday–Friday, 8am–3:30pm (last drop).

What Westminster city services won't take

  • No city trash pickup: "The City of Westminster does not provide citywide trash pickup service." Residents hire a licensed private hauler, so whether your hauler takes tires depends on that company. City of Westminster
  • Car tires at the Sustainability Center: the center's hard-to-recycle list includes bike tires and tubes but not car or truck tires. City of Westminster
  • Landfills: waste tires are banned from Colorado landfills. Denver Public Health & Environment

Other options

  • Search the city's Recycle Right Guide, call 303-658-2400, or email sustainabledisposal@westminsterco.gov for other places that take tires.
  • If you're buying new tires, ask the shop whether it takes your old ones. Fees vary, so call to confirm.
  • Have a lot of tires or no way to haul them? Colorado Tire Recyclers also offers pickup. Call to confirm the price.

FAQ

Does the City of Westminster pick up tires?

No. Westminster doesn't provide citywide trash pickup; residents hire a licensed private hauler. Ask your hauler whether it takes tires.

Can I drop off car tires at the Westminster Sustainability Center?

The center's list covers bike tires and tubes only, at $1.50 each (checked Oct 23, 2026). For car tires, call 303-593-0808 to confirm or use a tire recycler.

Where can I drop off car tires near Westminster?

Colorado Tire Recyclers at 11919 E 37th Ave, Denver, takes drop-offs Monday–Friday, 8am–3:30pm (last drop), and lists $3.25 per passenger tire, or $9.00 on the rim (checked Oct 23, 2026).
