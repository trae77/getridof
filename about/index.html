Every city in the Denver metro has different rules for tires, paint, mattresses and electronics. This site puts the answer for one item in one city on one page, with links to the official city, county, or facility source and the date we checked it.

We don't guess. If a fee or hour isn't published, we say "call to confirm." Rules change, so please confirm with the facility before you drive over.
